# Auto documentation

Auto turns how your team works into playbooks: reusable functions and workflows for one business responsibility, which you build with your agent. Auto Runtime runs them and records every run. Your agent is the primary interface for creating and using them; Auto Studio is the management interface for operational visibility and controls.

## Understand the model

Start with [Playbooks](concepts/playbooks.md) to see how a business responsibility becomes a playbook. [Functions and availability](concepts/functions.md) explains the types of work its functions can perform and their current status. [Workflows and runs](concepts/workflows.md) covers coordination, while [History and Dream](concepts/history-and-dream.md) explains recorded work and the proposed use of that history.

[Terminology](concepts/terminology.md) defines the shared vocabulary for the product, the documentation and the code.

## Build something

[Start Auto locally and connect your agent](get-started/local.md), then follow [Build your first playbook](tutorials/first-playbook.md). You do not need an Auto Cloud account. The tutorial creates a campaign-brief review function and compares two recorded runs.

Auto Cloud is currently invite-only. [Request an invitation](https://on.auto/request-invite) if you would prefer to run your playbooks on Auto Cloud.

To bring graph data into an existing function, follow [Use Auto with Apollo](integrations/apollo.md).
